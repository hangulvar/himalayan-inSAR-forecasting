# List of areas of interest for future analysis:

## 1. Chashoti village (Chositi)
The catastrophic landslide and flash flood hit Chashoti village (Chositi), located in the Machail (Padder) region of Kishtwar District, Jammu and Kashmir.The disaster unfolded along the rugged banks of the Bhazuti Nadi stream, situated roughly 19.5 kilometers deep into the mountain trail from Gulabgarh. It serves as a major rest stop before pilgrims transition to the final foot-trek portion toward the sacred Machail Mata (Chandi Mata) Temple.

## 2. Mata Vashino Devi
In August 2025, a devastating landslide hit the Mata Vaishno Devi shrine route in Jammu and Kashmir, near the base camp town of Katra. The deadly disaster occurred near the Inderprastha Bhojnalaya at Adhkwari, which is roughly halfway along the 12-kilometer trekking path from Katra.
